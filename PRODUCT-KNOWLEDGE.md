# Product knowledge review

Reviewed 8 November 2026 from public BASED pages for this independent concept. This is a provisional source audit, not medical advice or a BASED approval. The public preview is a deterministic offline simulation, not a trained language model; it makes no API calls.

The audit fetched 103 pages listed in the public sitemap, including products, collections, pages and guides. The product feed listed additional handles outside the sitemap; 11 could not be fetched during the batch, including Conditioner, which was separately reviewed through the public product page. Some are sets or accessories. The sitemap and feed are snapshots, not proof of every URL on the site. Full claim-by-claim verification of every page and variant remains outstanding.

The catalog stores source URLs, application directions, conditions, frequency where supported, and combinations. Missing directions stay marked unverified. The new hair-type and pairing answers are paraphrases of product pages and guides; claims of guaranteed outcomes, allergy safety, and medical benefits are deliberately excluded. Product instructions and availability can change.
