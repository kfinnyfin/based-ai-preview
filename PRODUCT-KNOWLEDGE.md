# Product knowledge review

Reviewed 8 November 2026 from public BASED pages for this independent concept. This is a provisional source audit, not medical advice or a BASED approval. The public preview is a deterministic offline simulation, not a trained language model; it makes no API calls.

The audit fetched 103 pages listed in the public sitemap, including products, collections, pages and guides. The product feed listed additional handles outside the sitemap; 11 could not be fetched during the batch, including Conditioner, which was separately reviewed through the public product page. Some are sets or accessories. The sitemap and feed are snapshots, not proof of every URL on the site. Full claim-by-claim verification of every page and variant remains outstanding.

The catalog stores source URLs, application directions, conditions, frequency where supported, and combinations. Missing directions stay marked unverified. The new hair-type and pairing answers are paraphrases of product pages and guides; claims of guaranteed outcomes, allergy safety, and medical benefits are deliberately excluded. Product instructions and availability can change.

## Follow-up research — 8 November 2026

The current public product pages were revisited for all 21 individual catalog entries; the Skin Elixir page required its explicit variant URL. The evidence snapshot in `PRODUCT-EVIDENCE.md` lists the demo's current directions, suitability, source links and missing fields for each entry. This is a working audit, not proof that all variants, source claims, or video demonstrations were independently verified. The four missing application instructions for Daily Facial Cleanser, Tallow Moisturizer, Body Wash and Deodorant remain unknown in the demo. Ingredient and availability snapshots require label verification.

The Conditioner product page identifies all hair types and lengths and describes daily use as an option. Curl Gel's FAQ gives a quarter-sized amount for medium-length hair and recommends wet or damp hair, sectioning, scrunching, and drying before scrunching out the cast. Skin Elixir's linked variant states no added fragrance. Marketing claims and individual safety assurances have not been treated as verified outcomes.

## Question coverage follow-up

The offline regression bank now exercises 12 product-specific question types for each of the 21 individual products, plus 20 goal and routine prompts. These are scripted test questions, not proof that all variations work or that every answer contains a verified instruction. Where the sources do not establish a frequency, amount, application method, scent, or product pairing, the answer says so and points to the product page. In particular, application directions for Daily Facial Cleanser, Tallow Moisturizer, Body Wash and Deodorant remain unverified in the reviewed source text. Rechecked product-page facts include Body Wash and Deodorant scent choices (subject to variants), Curl Cream and Shampoo scent descriptions, Hair Elixir bottle-life estimate, Texture Powder post-workout use, and Conditioner co-wash guidance.
